Color Sort

A calm sorting puzzle: pour colored liquids between tubes until every tube holds a single color.

As you finish levels, a bleached coral reef comes back to life. Play 1,000 levels, a daily puzzle and the Master mode, and unlock new reef areas, tube styles and liquid palettes along the way.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

A level seems impossible.

Every level has at least one solution. If you reach a dead end, the game tells you; you can undo, add an extra tube or start over. The bulb button shows the next good move.

How are stars counted?

The fewer moves you use, the more stars you earn. Solving a level in the shortest possible number of moves is a perfect solve.

Is there a color-blind mode?

Yes. Turn it on in Settings to show a shape on every color.

Which languages are supported?

English, Türkçe, Português (Brasil), Español, Bahasa Indonesia, Tiếng Việt, ไทย and Bahasa Melayu. The game follows your phone's language; you can change it in Settings.

Are there ads or purchases?

The game is free and shows ads (Google AdMob). There are no in-app purchases. You can watch an optional ad for a hint, an extra tube, extra undos or moves, or bonus coins. See the Privacy Policy.

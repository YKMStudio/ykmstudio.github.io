About YKM Studio

We are a small, independent studio making mobile games that are quick to start and hard to put down.

What we make

Our games are short-session puzzle and arcade games for iPhone: block puzzles, sorting and logic puzzles, and one-tap arcade games. Each one is built around a single simple rule that keeps getting deeper the more you play.

How we make them

  • Calm and fair. No timers unless you want them, puzzles that always have a solution, and help when you get stuck.
  • Free to play. Our games are free. Some show ads to keep them free; we keep ads away from the middle of a game wherever we can.
  • Light on data. No accounts and no sign-in. Your progress stays on your device, and we only use anonymous statistics to find bugs and improve levels. Each game's privacy policy says exactly what it uses.
  • For everyone. Our games are translated into several languages and are suitable for all ages.

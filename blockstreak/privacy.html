Privacy Policy

Block Streak: Puzzle Game · Effective date: October 18, 2026 · Türkçe

Block Streak: Puzzle Game ("the game") is developed and published by YKM Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • No accounts, no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or photos.
  • We collect anonymous gameplay statistics to improve the game.
  • The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. There are no in-app purchases (coins are earned in the game only).
  • Ads use your device's advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when the game asks.
  • We do not sell your data.

Information we collect

We use services from Google Firebase:

  • Firebase Analytics records when the game is opened and how long it is played, and game events such as starting a game, finishing the tutorial and the final score, together with general device information (device model, operating system version, app version, language, country) and a random app-instance identifier created by Firebase. This identifier is not linked to your identity.

We use this information only to understand how the game is played, improve it and fix bugs. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the game.

Google processes this data on our behalf and may store it on servers outside your country, including in the United States. See Google's privacy policy and Firebase privacy information.

Information that stays on your device

Your best score, coins, the skins you own and use, the number of games you played, achievements, settings (sound, music, vibration), language choice, and whether you finished the tutorial are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules one reminder a day, in the early evening, directly on your device. No server sends them and no data is sent to us for this. You can turn reminders off in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings at any time.

Game Center

On iPhone, the game can sign you in to Apple's Game Center to show the leaderboard and achievements. Your best score and achievements are then sent to Game Center under your Game Center nickname. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we do not receive your Apple ID.

Ads

The game shows a banner on the home screen, a full-screen ad from time to time (at most after every second lost game, and not more often than every three minutes), and, only if you choose to, a video ad that lets you continue a lost game. Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK receives information such as your IP address (used to estimate your approximate location, e.g. country or city), device model, operating system version, app version, language, an identifier for vendors or other device identifier, and how you interact with ads (for example impressions and taps). This information is not linked to your identity by us.

On iPhone, the game asks for permission before ads may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to personalize ads ("Allow Tracking"). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you still see ads, but they are not personalized with that identifier. You can change this at any time in your phone's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Google acts as an independent controller for the data it uses for advertising. See how Google uses information in ads and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where required by law, the game shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) on first launch so you can choose whether your data may be used for ads and measurement. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy Options. If you do not consent, you will see non-personalized or limited ads.

The game engine

The game is built with the Unity engine by Unity Technologies. Unity may receive anonymous technical information about the device the game runs on (for example the device model and operating system version) to support and improve the engine. This information does not identify you and is not used for advertising or tracking. See Unity's privacy policy for game players.

Sharing

If you tap "Share with friends", your device's own share sheet opens with a short message. You choose whether and where to send it; the game does not see what you send.

Data retention

Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months, after which they are deleted automatically. Advertising data is retained by Google according to its own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or Turkey's KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete data about you, to object to its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because the data we receive is not linked to your identity, the simplest way to stop all collection and reset the identifier is to delete the app. You can also limit ad data through Settings → Privacy Options in the game (where available). For any request, email us at the address below.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version; significant changes will be announced in the app or on this page before they take effect.

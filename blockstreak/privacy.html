Block Streak Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Block Streak is a block puzzle game made by YKM Studio. This policy explains what happens to information when you play it. In short: we collect only anonymous usage statistics to improve the game, never your name, contacts or location, and we never track you for advertising.

What we collect

Block Streak has no accounts, no advertising and no in-app purchases. To learn whether players enjoy the game and come back to it, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase, which receives anonymous usage statistics:

  • when the game is opened and how long it is played,
  • game events such as starting a game, finishing the tutorial and the final score,
  • basic device information (device model, operating system version, language, country derived from the connection) and a random app installation identifier.

This information is not linked to your name, email or any account, does not include the advertising identifier, and is not used to track you across other apps or websites. We do not sell or share it. See Google's privacy policy and Firebase privacy information.

What is stored on your device

The game saves a few things on your own device so it can remember them between sessions:

  • your best score,
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration),
  • whether you have finished the tutorial,
  • whether you were asked about reminder notifications.

This information stays on your device. Deleting the game deletes it.

Reminder notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules occasional reminders on your own device, for example a day after you last played. They are created by the game on the device itself; no server sends them. You can turn them off at any time in your device's settings.

Game Center

On iPhone, the game can sign you in to Apple's Game Center to show the leaderboard. Your best score is then sent to Game Center under your Game Center nickname. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we do not receive your Apple ID.

The game engine

Block Streak is built with the Unity engine by Unity Technologies. Unity may receive anonymous technical information about the device the game runs on (for example the device model and operating system version) to support and improve the engine. This information does not identify you and is not used for advertising or tracking. See Unity's privacy policy for game players.

Children

Block Streak is suitable for all ages. We do not collect personal information that identifies anyone, including children.

Sharing

If you tap "Share with friends", your device's own share sheet opens with a short message. You choose whether and where to send it; the game does not see what you send.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

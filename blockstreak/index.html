Block Streak

A relaxing 9x9 block puzzle. Fill rows, columns and 3x3 squares, chain your clears and keep the streak alive.

Drag pieces onto the board and fill complete rows, columns or 3x3 squares to clear them. Clear on back-to-back moves to build your Flow streak: the longer it runs, the more every clear is worth. Pieces can't be rotated, and the game ends when none of them fit.

Contact us

Found a problem or have a question? Write to ykm.studio.development@gmail.com. Please include your device model and iOS version. We usually answer within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Drag one of the three pieces below the board onto an empty spot. When a row, a column or a 3x3 square is completely filled, it clears and you score points. The game ends when none of your pieces fits on the board. You can replay the tutorial from Settings > More > How to play on the home screen.

What is Flow?

Every move that clears something raises your Flow by one. The higher your Flow, the more each clear is worth. A move that clears nothing resets Flow to zero.

Can I rotate pieces?

No. Pieces are placed as they are shown.

Can I continue after a lost game?

Once per game you can watch a short video ad to get three new pieces and keep your board and score.

How do I get new skins?

You earn coins at the end of every game. Spend them under Skins on the home screen to unlock new block colors.

Are there ads or purchases?

The game is free and shows ads (Google AdMob). There are no in-app purchases. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I turn off sound, music or vibration, or change the language?

Yes, in Settings (the gear button). Your choices are remembered.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Block Streak can be played offline. Ads need a connection.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game?

Yes. Your best score, coins, skins and settings are stored only on your device.

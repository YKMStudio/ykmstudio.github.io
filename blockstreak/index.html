Block Streak Support

Block Streak is a relaxing 9x9 block puzzle. Drag pieces onto the board, fill rows, columns and 3x3 squares to clear them, and clear on back-to-back moves to build your Flow streak and multiply your points.

Contact us

Found a problem or have a question? Write to ykm.studio.support@gmail.com. Please include your device model and iOS version. We usually answer within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Drag one of the three pieces below the board onto an empty spot. When a row, a column or a 3x3 square is completely filled, it clears and you score points. The game ends when none of your pieces fits on the board. You can replay the tutorial from Settings > More > How to play on the home screen.

What is Flow?

Every move that clears something raises your Flow by one. The higher your Flow, the more each clear is worth. A move that clears nothing resets Flow to zero.

Can I rotate pieces?

No. Pieces are placed as they are shown.

Can I turn off sound, music or vibration?

Yes, in Settings (the gear button). Your choice is remembered.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Block Streak works fully offline.

Will I lose my best score if I delete the game?

Yes. Your best score and settings are stored only on your device.

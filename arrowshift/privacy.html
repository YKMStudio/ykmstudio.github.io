Privacy Policy

Arrow Shift · Effective date: October 18, 2026 · Türkçe

Arrow Shift ("the game") is developed and published by YKM Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • No accounts, no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or photos.
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting services, and the game does not send us any data.
  • The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. There are no in-app purchases (coins are earned in the game only).
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and the game does not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • We do not sell your data.

Information we collect

The game does not use analytics or crash reporting services and does not send any information about how you play to us. The only data that leaves your device is what the advertising service needs to show ads (see Ads below).

Information that stays on your device

Your level progress, hearts, hints, coins, skins, settings and language choice are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Ads

The game shows a banner ad on the home screen, a full-screen ad between some levels, and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch for an extra heart or hint. Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK receives information such as your IP address (used to estimate your approximate location, e.g. country or city), device model, operating system version, app version, language, an identifier for vendors or other device identifier, and how you interact with ads (for example impressions and taps). This information is not linked to your identity by us.

The game does not ask for tracking permission and does not access the IDFA, so ads are not personalized based on your activity in other companies' apps or websites. Ads may still be selected based on the context and your approximate location.

Google acts as an independent controller for the data it uses for advertising. See how Google uses information in ads and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where required by law, the game shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) on first launch so you can choose whether your data may be used for ads and measurement. To change your choice later, delete and reinstall the game to see the form again, or contact us. If you do not consent, you will see non-personalized or limited ads.

The game engine

The game is built with the Unity engine by Unity Technologies. Unity may receive anonymous technical information about the device the game runs on (for example the device model and operating system version) to support and improve the engine. This information does not identify you and is not used for advertising or tracking. See Unity's privacy policy for game players.

Data retention

Advertising data is retained by Google according to its own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or Turkey's KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete data about you, to object to its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because the data we receive is not linked to your identity, the simplest way to stop all collection is to delete the app. For any request, email us at the address below.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version; significant changes will be announced in the app or on this page before they take effect.

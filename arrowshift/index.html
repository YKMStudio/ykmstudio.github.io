Meow Arrows

Untangle the yarn arrows in the right order and rescue the cats caught in them.

Each yarn arrow slides off the board in the direction its head points, but only if its path is clear. Every level is a picture drawn with yarn; clear it to reveal the cat hiding underneath. Rescue each cat level by level, meet locks and pulse gates along the way, and come back for a new daily puzzle.

Frequently asked questions

Why won't an arrow move?

Something is in its way. A blocked arrow can't leave; the game marks what is in the way. Clear that first.

What happens when I run out of hearts?

Every level starts with three hearts. When you run out, you can watch a short ad to get a heart back or start the level over.

How do I get hints?

You start with one hint and can hold up to three. Need another? Watch a short ad for a hint.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Are there ads or purchases?

The game is free and shows ads (Google AdMob). One optional in-app purchase, "Remove ads", turns off the banner and the ads between levels; ads you choose to watch for a heart, a hint or extra coins stay available. Coins and skins are earned in the game only. See the Privacy Policy.

I bought "Remove ads" on another device or reinstalled. How do I get it back?

Open Settings and tap "Restore purchases" while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Why does the game send notifications?

If you allow them, the game sends a local reminder when a new daily puzzle is ready or a cat is waiting. They are scheduled on your device, not sent from a server. You can turn them off in your device's Settings.

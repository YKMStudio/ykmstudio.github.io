Arrow Shift

Tap winding arrows to slide them off the board. Find the right order and clear them all.

An arrow leaves the board in the direction its head points, but only if its path is clear. Work out which arrow has to go first, and meet locks, one-way gates and pulse gates as you move through the chapters.

Frequently asked questions

Why won't an arrow move?

Something is in its way. A blocked arrow can't leave; the game marks what is in the way. Clear that first.

What happens when I run out of hearts?

Every level starts with three hearts. When you run out, you can watch a short ad to get a heart back or start the level over.

How do I get hints?

Every completed level gives you a hint. Need one now? Watch a short ad for another hint.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Are there ads or purchases?

The game is free and shows ads (Google AdMob). There are no in-app purchases. See the Privacy Policy.

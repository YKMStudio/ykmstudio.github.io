Flow Dots

A calm, glowing logic puzzle. Draw pipes to connect every pair of matching dots and fill the whole board.

Play 1,600+ levels in 11 packs, six new Daily Puzzles every day, a themed Weekly Challenge and Time Trial. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I get more hints?

You start with 3 hints and receive one free hint every day. When you run out, you can watch a short ad to get another hint.

A level seems impossible.

Every puzzle has exactly one solution, and every cell must be filled. A hint reveals one pipe.

How do I earn a star?

Solve a level in the fewest possible moves.

Are there ads or purchases?

The game is free and shows ads (Google AdMob). There are no in-app purchases. See the Privacy Policy.

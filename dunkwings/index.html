Dunk Wings

Tap to fly a winged ball through the hoops. Swish in a row and set it on fire.

Every tap makes the ball flap upward. Drop it through each hoop from above to score. Go through without touching the rim for a swish: chain swishes for DOUBLE, TRIPLE and bigger combos that multiply your points. Climb the ranks from Noob to Master and unlock new balls, wings, hoops, fire trails and hats.

Frequently asked questions

When does the game end?

When a hoop passes you without the ball going through it, or when the ball falls off the bottom of the screen. Touching the rim does not end the game; it only resets your combo.

How do I unlock skins?

Skins are unlocked by rank. Each new rank you reach with your best score unlocks one new item in every category.

Can I continue after I miss?

Once per game you can watch a short video ad to continue with the same score.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your best score, rank and skins.

Are there ads or purchases?

The game is free and shows ads (Google AdMob). There are no in-app purchases. See the Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy

Farm Mow: Maze Puzzle · Effective date: October 24, 2026 · Türkçe

Farm Mow: Maze Puzzle ("the game") is developed and published by YKM Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • No accounts, no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or photos.
  • We do not collect analytics or crash reports. The game sends no data to us.
  • The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Optional in-app purchases (remove ads, coin packs) are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and the game does not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • We do not sell your data.

Information that stays on your device

Your level, coins, the vehicles and farm items you unlocked, your settings (sound, vibration, language, season) and tutorial progress are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

In-app purchases

You can buy "Remove Ads" and coin packs. Payments are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never receive your name, payment card or Apple ID. To deliver a purchase, the game receives a transaction record from the App Store, which is checked and used on your device only. Whether you removed ads is stored on your device and can be restored with "Restore purchases" in the shop.

Ads

The game shows a banner ad on the main menu, a full-screen ad at most once every ten levels, and, only if you choose to, a video ad that skips a level, refills fuel or gives you coins. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. If you buy "Remove Ads", the banner and full-screen ads stop; optional videos stay available.

To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK receives information such as your IP address (used to estimate your approximate location, e.g. country or city), device model, operating system version, app version, language, an identifier for vendors or other device identifier, and how you interact with ads (for example impressions and taps). This information is not linked to your identity by us.

The game does not ask for tracking permission and does not access the IDFA, so ads are not personalized based on your activity in other companies' apps or websites. Ads may still be selected based on the context and your approximate location.

Google acts as an independent controller for the data it uses for advertising. See how Google uses information in ads and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where required by law, the game shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) on first launch so you can choose whether your data may be used for ads and measurement. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy Options. If you do not consent, you will see non-personalized or limited ads.

The game engine

The game is built with the Unity engine by Unity Technologies. Unity may receive anonymous technical information about the device the game runs on (for example the device model and operating system version) to support and improve the engine. This information does not identify you and is not used for advertising or tracking. See Unity's privacy policy for game players.

Data retention

We do not receive or store any data about you. Advertising data is retained by Google according to its own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or Turkey's KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete data about you, to object to its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. We hold no data about you: your progress lives only on your device and is removed when you delete the app. You can limit ad data through Settings → Privacy Options in the game (where available). For any request, email us at the address below.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version; significant changes will be announced in the app or on this page before they take effect.

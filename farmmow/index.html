Farm Mow

Swipe your mower through a farm maze and mow every patch of grass.

Each swipe drives your vehicle until it hits a wall, mowing the grass it passes. Plan your route so no patch is left behind, then the next field rolls in. Earn coins, unlock new vehicles from a robot mower to a combine harvester, grow your own farm with a barn, animals and a windmill, and play through four seasons.

Contact us

Found a bug or have an idea? Email mekaxstudio@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS version and the level number.

Frequently asked questions

I'm stuck on a level. What can I do?

Every level can always be finished, whatever moves you made before. Try a different direction first, or watch a short video to skip the level.

How do I get new vehicles and farm items?

Every level gives you coins, and coin levels give extra. Spend them in the Garage for vehicles and in My Farm for farm items. You can also watch a video for coins a few times a day.

Can I choose the season?

Yes. The season changes every 100 levels by default; you can pick summer, autumn, winter or spring in Settings → Season.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your level, coins, vehicles and farm.

What is fuel?

Every swipe uses one fuel. The tank is generous and always enough to finish the level. If you run out, fill up with coins, watch a short video for half a tank, or start the level again for free. Bigger vehicles have bigger tanks.

Are there ads or purchases?

The game is free and shows ads (Google AdMob). You can buy "Remove Ads" and coin packs in the shop; "Restore purchases" brings back Remove Ads on a new device. See the Privacy Policy.
